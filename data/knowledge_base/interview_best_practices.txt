Interview Best Practices — Backend / Platform Engineering Roles

TECHNICAL QUESTIONS
Favor questions that reveal how a candidate reasons about trade-offs
rather than pure trivia. For backend roles, cover: API design choices,
database schema/indexing decisions, handling of failure modes (timeouts,
retries, idempotency), and horizontal scaling strategies. Ask candidates
to walk through a system they built end-to-end, including what they would
change with hindsight.

BEHAVIORAL QUESTIONS
Use the STAR framework (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Good behavioral
questions for engineering roles probe: handling disagreement with a
teammate or manager, a time a project went wrong and what they learned,
how they've mentored or supported a junior colleague, and how they
prioritize when everything feels urgent.

GAP-PROBING QUESTIONS
When a candidate's resume shows a gap relative to the required skills
(e.g., limited Kubernetes exposure for a role that requires it), ask a
scenario-based question in that area rather than a yes/no question. This
reveals whether the candidate can reason about the concept even without
deep hands-on experience, and avoids screening out strong learners.

RED FLAGS TO AVOID IN QUESTIONS
Do not ask about protected characteristics (age, family status, religion,
disability, etc.). Keep all questions job-related and consistent across
candidates for the same role to keep the process fair and legally sound.

SCORING GUIDANCE
Score technical depth, communication clarity, and collaboration signals
separately rather than collapsing everything into one number, so the
hiring team can see *why* a candidate scored the way they did.
