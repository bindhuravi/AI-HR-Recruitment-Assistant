Senior Backend Engineer
Company: Skyline Technologies

ABOUT THE ROLE
We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to design and scale the
services powering our core product. You'll work closely with product and
platform teams to build reliable, well-tested APIs and distributed
systems.

REQUIRED SKILLS
- Python (5+ years)
- Experience designing REST APIs
- PostgreSQL or another relational database
- Docker and Kubernetes in production
- AWS or another major cloud provider
- Strong understanding of distributed systems and system design

PREFERRED SKILLS
- Go
- Experience with event-driven architectures (Kafka, SQS)
- Experience mentoring junior engineers

MINIMUM EXPERIENCE
5 years of professional backend engineering experience

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Design, build, and operate backend services that scale to millions of
  requests per day
- Own service reliability: monitoring, alerting, on-call rotation
- Collaborate with product managers to translate requirements into
  technical designs
- Mentor mid-level and junior engineers on the team

QUALIFICATIONS
- B.S. in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience
- Track record of shipping and operating production systems at scale
